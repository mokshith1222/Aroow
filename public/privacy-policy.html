Privacy Policy for Aroow

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Welcome to Aroow ("the App"), developed by independent developers ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information handled through your use of the App.

Summary: Aroow is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, require, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, address, or phone number. All game progress and settings remain strictly stored locally on your device.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores certain data strictly locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage:

  • Game Progression: Unlocked levels, completed worlds, best star ratings (1 to 3 stars), best move counts, and completion times.
  • Player Preferences: Audio volume, sound effects toggle (ON/OFF), background music toggle (ON/OFF), and haptic vibration feedback preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any external server owned by us. You can clear this data at any time via the in-game Settings menu or through your Android device settings (Apps > Aroow > Storage > Clear Storage).

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To keep the App free to play, we utilize Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve advertisements (interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads). Third-party advertising partners may collect or receive certain device information:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): Used by Google AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.
  • Device Identifiers & Telemetry: Device type, operating system version, carrier, and general diagnostic performance metrics.
  • IP Address: Used for coarse geographic location to serve region-relevant ads and language matching.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Partner Terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. In-App Telemetry & Analytics

The App includes lightweight, anonymous telemetry to monitor puzzle difficulty balance and crash rates (such as level completion rates and session durations). No personal data or user identity is tracked.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Aroow is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

5. Security

All communications between third-party advertising SDKs and their servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

Since we do not maintain accounts or store personal data on remote servers, you maintain complete control over your data directly on your device:

  • You can reset all progress within the App via Settings > Reset Progress.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to permanently remove all locally stored game files.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published here with an updated "Effective Date".
