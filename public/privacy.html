AROOW PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Aroow. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Aroow mobile application ("Aroow" or "the Game").

Aroow is primarily an offline puzzle game. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to play the Game.

1. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

Aroow does not require you to provide:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account passwords or login credentials
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Photos

Aroow does not operate a user account system or maintain a personal user profile on its own servers.

2. GAME DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Aroow stores certain game information locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved.

This may include:

  • Level progression
  • Stars
  • Coins
  • Purchased cosmetics
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Gameplay statistics generated by the Game

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted by Aroow to our own servers or cloud database.

Aroow's locally stored gameplay statistics may include information such as session duration, levels played, level completions and similar gameplay metrics. These statistics remain on your device.

If you uninstall Aroow or clear the application's data, locally stored game information may be permanently deleted.

3. ADVERTISING

Aroow is supported by advertising.

The Game uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

Depending on applicable privacy requirements, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address and network-related information
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • Information used to provide, measure, limit or personalize advertising where permitted

This information is processed by Google and its advertising services in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

Aroow itself does not directly access or store your Android Advertising ID.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. PRIVACY CHOICES AND CONSENT

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, Aroow may display a privacy or consent message when advertising services are used.

Where applicable, you can make choices regarding the use of information for advertising.

You can also manage available advertising privacy choices through:

Aroow → Settings → Privacy → Privacy Choices

The availability of particular privacy options may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Aroow is intended as a general-audience game.

Aroow does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Advertising and privacy settings are configured according to the applicable requirements of the advertising services and the settings selected for the application.

Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy or advertising in Aroow can contact us using the contact information below.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Aroow does not maintain your game progression on our own servers.

Game data stored locally on your device remains there until it is deleted by you, such as by clearing the application's data or uninstalling Aroow.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to those services' respective policies and retention practices.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Aroow currently uses Google AdMob for advertising.

Google may process information in connection with advertising according to its own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy when Aroow's features, services or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this policy.
