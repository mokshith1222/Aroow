Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Aroow! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Aroow mobile application ("the Game").

Aroow is primarily an offline game. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect or store personal profiles on our servers.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any of the following personal information:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Account passwords or login credentials
  • Precise location data (GPS)
  • Contacts or photos

2. Local Game Data (Stored on Your Device)

Your game progression, unlocked levels, stars, coins, cosmetics, and settings are stored entirely locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers or any cloud database. We also collect anonymized gameplay analytics (such as session length and level completions) that remain strictly on your device.

Note: If you uninstall the game or clear the app's local storage, this data will be permanently lost.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

To keep Aroow free to play, we use third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdMob. These services may collect and process certain information to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

The information collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse network location
  • Usage data and crash logs related to ad delivery

This data is handled directly by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Privacy Choices and Consent

Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area, UK, or specific US states), you may be presented with a consent form when you first launch the game. You can manage your advertising preferences and consent choices at any time by navigating to Settings > Privacy Choices within the app.

5. Future Notifications

Aroow may introduce local reminder notifications in the future. These notifications will be scheduled entirely on your device, and no notification data will be sent to external servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Aroow is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Any data collection related to advertising is strictly governed by the age-appropriateness settings configured in our ad networks.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your game data is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can delete all your game data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing its data via your device's system settings.
